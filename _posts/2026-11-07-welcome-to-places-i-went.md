Welcome to **Places I Went** — a home for the places, experiences and discoveries I'd like to remember and share.

This is the start of a growing collection of travel stories. You'll find destination guides, candid hotel reviews, places to eat, and practical notes that are useful when planning your own trips.

## What you'll find here

I want to write about what a place actually feels like: the neighborhood around a hotel, the breakfast that was worth getting up for, or a small detour that turned into a highlight.

## An evolving travel notebook

Some articles will be long guides with photographs and recommendations. Others will be short observations or reviews. All of them will be based on actual experiences, rather than generic travel lists.

More stories to come. Until then, welcome aboard.
