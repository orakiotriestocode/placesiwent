Development log

Built in public, playable as we go.

Alpha 0.2 · First public build: world exploration, ships and colonists, settlements, early production, trade and browser saves. Expect prototype rough edges; mechanics, artwork and original audio compositions will evolve in later releases.

Now in Alpha 1.1: pixel-styled colony buildings, upgraded European market and cargo controls, multiple settler professions, and recruitment through immigration. Existing field and workshop assignments remain playable. All artwork and music will be original or appropriately licensed.
