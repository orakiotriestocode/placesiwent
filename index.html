About Travel Ramblings

Travel Ramblings starts as a collection of personal travel notes, recommendations and discoveries.

Over time, it may grow into a travel wiki containing detailed pages about hotels, restaurants, landmarks, airlines, transport options and destinations.

The longer-term goal is to combine these articles with geolocation. A visitor could open the app, allow access to their location and discover interesting places nearby, including their history, practical information and personal recommendations.
